# Live Demo — Classification Guide

**Stage: Emerging.** Seen in only a few ads so far. Promote to Established (change `stage:` above and drop "Emerging." in the index) once it shows up from 3 different brands.

(Added October 6th, 2026, approved by Alysha. Media: video.)

## Definition

Someone causes the problem on camera, and the product visibly reacts to it and fixes it, so the proof plays out live in front of you.

## The things that must all be true

1. **The problem is created on purpose, on camera** ("watch this, I'm gonna spray...", pour, spill, smudge).
2. **The product visibly responds** with something you can see change: a reading or light, a stain lifting, water beading off.
3. **The before-and-after happens in one continuous test**, not as separate staged shots. The test is the spine of the ad; other beats (feature list, lifestyle b-roll) hang off it.

## Quick test

Could you describe the ad as "they did X to it and watched it fix it"? If yes, Live Demo.

## Commonly confused with

- **Explainer:** teaches you something you'd learn even if you skipped the product. Live Demo is all about proving the product works.
- **Before-and-after:** a matched pair of results (scalp, skin) as proof. Live Demo shows the change happening in a test the ad sets up itself.
- **Transformation:** a problem-to-result story told across separate shots. Live Demo is one live test.
- **ASMR / unboxing:** sound or reveal is the hook, not a test.

## Example

Sans air purifier: a presenter sprays household cleaner beside the unit, the number jumps and the light turns red, a "Sans can trap..." feature run, then the light returns to blue and the number falls. Examples live in `../../files/` (match `vf=live-demo` in the filename).
