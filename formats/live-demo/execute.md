# Live Demo — Implementation SOP

## What it is

Someone causes the problem on camera, and the product visibly reacts to it and fixes it, so the proof plays out live.

## Build rules

1. Open on the provocation: "Watch this" plus the action in the first 2 seconds.
2. Pick a problem that's everyday and believable (household cleaner, coffee spill), not a lab stunt.
3. Make the product's reaction readable on a phone: tight close-up on the display, light, or surface.
4. Name what's happening while it happens ("that color is gonna change").
5. Fill the waiting time with a fast benefit run or lifestyle b-roll, then cut back to the result.
6. Close on the fix landing (back to normal), then the product hero and CTA.

## QA checklist

- [ ] The test is real and the change is visible, not implied
- [ ] Hook is the provocation, not the logo
- [ ] Result is shown on camera before the end card
- [ ] Honest claims; no absolutes or banned phrases
