# Street Interview — Classification Guide

(Named by Alysha and promoted from the parking lot, 2026-09-30. Media: video.)

## Definition

A video where **people are stopped on the street** (or in public) and asked questions on camera, often a quiz, a taste test or a stunt, with the product woven in.

## The things that must all be true

1. **Public setting** (street, mall, event).
2. **Strangers stopped and asked** on camera.
3. **The product is woven into the questions or stunt.**

## Quick test

Are passers-by being stopped and asked questions? If yes, Street Interview.

## Commonly confused with

- **Interview:** a planned interview, not strangers in public.
- **Taste Test:** if the whole piece is about tasting, check Taste Test.

## Signals

Handheld mic, passers-by, city background, reactions.
