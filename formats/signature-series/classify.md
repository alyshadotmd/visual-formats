# Signature Series — Classification Guide

**Stage: Emerging.** Seen in only a few ads so far. Promote to Established (change `stage:` above and drop "Emerging." in the index) once it shows up from 3 different brands.

## Reference examples (look here first)

To find examples, look in the shared `../../files/` folder for any file whose name contains `vf=signature-series`.

## Definition

A brand hires a creator to make one more episode of the creator's own recurring series, done exactly the way the creator always does it (same setup, same bit, same voice), with the product written into the bit.

## The things that must all be true

1. The creator has a known recurring series (a repeat premise their audience recognizes, like "can you do X finding out about Y").
2. The ad is an episode of that series: same setup, same style of joke, same filming look as their unpaid episodes.
3. The brand fits inside the premise (the brand requests the episode, or the product becomes part of the bit). The series stays the hero and the product rides along.

## Quick test

Ask: *would the creator's followers recognize this as just another episode?* If yes, Signature Series.

## Commonly confused with

- **Skit:** a scripted scene with 2+ characters written for the ad. Signature Series is the creator's own existing premise, with any number of characters.
- **Yapper / ugc:** a generic creator talking to camera. Signature Series is tied to one creator's recognizable series.
- **Comment-response:** often stacked with it, because fans (and brands) request episodes in the comments. Comment-response is the reply device; Signature Series is the premise being replied with.

## Signals

A brand's request shows up as a comment on the creator's own post rather than a sponsor brief, the episode matches the creator's unpaid episodes in setup, joke style, filming look, voice and timing, and any offer or code sits in the caption rather than inside the episode itself.
