# Explainer Format — Implementation SOP

## Reference examples (look here first)

They live in the shared `../../files/evergreen/` and `../../files/bfcm/` folders, tagged `vf=explainer`. Study them first.

## What this format is

A video where **someone who knows the topic explains something to you with confidence**. They may be a trusted expert, or just a knowledgeable everyday person. The ad teaches first (a comparison, a how-it-works, a myth cleared up), and the product fits into what you just learned.

## Build rules (priority order)

1. Pick one thing to teach that your buyer gets wrong.
2. Show it with props or visuals, not just talk.
3. Keep the tone confident and simple.
4. Bring the product in as the easy answer to the lesson.

## QA checklist

- [ ] One person is teaching the viewer something, with confidence
- [ ] The content is a lesson (how much, how it works, why), often with props or on-screen visuals
- [ ] The product fits into the lesson, not a straight pitch
- [ ] Honest claims; no absolutes or banned phrases
