# Offer Banner Format — Implementation SOP

## What this format is

A branded ad built like a sale banner: an image of the product (or the product lineup, or the bonus items) with a **big banner or big text that emphasizes the offer** ("26% Off Your First Subscription Order", "Lowest price yet", "UP TO 40% OFF everything", a large struck-through price). The offer is what you read first; the products support it.

## Build rules (priority order)

1. Headline = the offer, as big as the layout allows. One offer, stated plainly.
2. Products below or beside it, clean cut-outs or a studio shot, supporting not competing.
3. One brand-color or gradient background.
4. Small CTA ("Shop now") and required fine print.
5. Thumb-size check: the offer reads at a glance.

## QA checklist

- [ ] The offer is the hero
- [ ] Products are shown in support, in a studio or designed setting (seamless or gradient backdrop, brand-color field, a frame), not in a real-life scene someone would post
- [ ] No stronger device takes over
- [ ] Honest claims; no absolutes or banned phrases
