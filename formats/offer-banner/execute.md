# Offer Banner Format — Implementation SOP

## Reference examples (look here first)

To find examples, look in the shared `../../files/` folder for any file whose name contains `vf=offer-banner`.

## What this format is

A branded ad built like a sale banner: an image of the product (or the product lineup, or the bonus items) with a **big banner or big text that emphasizes the offer** ("26% Off Your First Subscription Order", "Lowest price yet", "UP TO 40% OFF everything", a large struck-through price). The offer is what you read first; the products support it.

Strongest for **sitewide and seasonal sales (Black Friday, Cyber Monday, subscription promos) and any moment where the discount or price itself is the strongest hook.**

The one property to protect: **the offer stays the hero.** Cover the products with your hand and the ad should still work as a sale announcement; if a stronger device (a receipt, repeated stacked type, native story text, a callout diagram) takes over, that device's format wins instead.

## Inputs

Expect the **offer** (the discount, price, or promo name and its terms) and the **product imagery** to support it (a single product, the lineup, or bonus items). The angle/promo supplies the offer itself and any fine print; this SOP governs how big it reads and how the products sit around it.

## Anatomy

1. **The offer headline:** the biggest or first-read text on the ad — a %, a $ amount, a struck-through price, or a sale name ("Black Friday Sale", "Lowest price yet").
2. **Product imagery:** the product, lineup, or bonus items shown in support, in a studio or designed setting (seamless or gradient backdrop, brand-color field, a frame) or a real-life/lifestyle photo, as long as the offer stays the hero.
3. **Background:** one brand-color or gradient field tying the layout together.
4. **CTA and fine print:** a small call to action ("Shop now") plus any required terms.

## Build rules (priority order)

1. **Headline = the offer,** as big as the layout allows. One offer, stated plainly.
2. **Products below or beside it,** clean cut-outs or a studio shot (or a supporting lifestyle photo), supporting not competing.
3. **One brand-color or gradient background.**
4. **Small CTA ("Shop now") and required fine print.**
5. **No stronger device takes over** the layout (a receipt, stacked type, native story text, or a callout diagram would each be their own format).
6. **Specific, real claims. No absolutes or banned phrases.**

## Length caps

| Element | Cap |
|---|---|
| Offer headline | ≤8 words, as large as the layout allows |
| CTA | 1 short line (2-3 words) |
| Fine print | As short as the offer's terms require |

## Build steps

1. Read the brief: the offer and its terms, and the product imagery available.
2. Write the offer headline, stated plainly as the single biggest text.
3. Choose the product imagery (single product, lineup, or bonus items) and its setting.
4. Lock one brand-color or gradient background tying it together.
5. Add the CTA and required fine print.
6. Thumb-size check: confirm the offer reads at a glance.
7. Claims pass (offer terms accurate; no absolutes or banned phrases).
8. Deliver: offer headline, product imagery direction, background, CTA, fine print, claims flags.

## QA checklist

- [ ] The offer is the hero
- [ ] Products are shown in support, in a studio or designed setting (seamless or gradient backdrop, brand-color field, a frame) or a supporting lifestyle photo, not a real-life scene that overtakes the offer
- [ ] No stronger device takes over
- [ ] The offer reads at a glance at thumb size
- [ ] Honest claims; no absolutes or banned phrases
