# Offer Banner — Classification Guide

(Named by Alysha and promoted from the parking lot, 2026-09-30. Media: static or video.)

## Definition

A branded ad built like a sale banner: an image of the product (or the product lineup, or the bonus items) with a **big banner or big text that emphasizes the offer** ("26% Off Your First Subscription Order", "Lowest price yet", "UP TO 40% OFF everything", a large struck-through price). The offer is what you read first; the products support it.

## The things that must all be true

1. **The offer is the hero.** The biggest or first-read text is the offer, sale or price (a %, a $ amount, a struck-through price, "Black Friday Sale", "Lowest price yet").
2. **Products are shown in support.** The background can be a studio or designed setting (seamless or gradient backdrop, brand-color field, a frame) or a real-life or lifestyle photo, as long as the offer text sits on top as the hero. (Alysha's calls, Sept 30 2026: Hommey lifestyle photos under a big offer are Offer Banner.) Flatlay and Shelfie win only when the real-life photo is the point and the offer is secondary.
3. **No stronger device takes over.** If the ad is built around a specific device (repeated stacked type, a receipt, a sticky note, native Instagram story text, a callout diagram), that device's format wins.

## Quick test

Cover the products with your hand. Does the ad still work as a sale announcement? If yes, Offer Banner.

## Commonly confused with

- **Headline about the product with a small offer** (a benefit or message headline, the offer only in a subline or badge): judge the device. There is no separate product-image format (retired by Alysha, 2026-09-30); most of these are still Offer Banner because the offer is the selling point, unless another format fits better.
- **Listicle:** a list is the hero. When a list only shows what's in the bundle under a big offer headline, it's Offer Banner (Buoy, 2026-09-30).
- **Text Echo:** the offer phrase repeated in stacked type is Text Echo.
- **Receipt:** the offer printed on a paper receipt, ticket or coupon is Receipt.
- **Flatlay / Shelfie:** those need a real-life setting (an Instagram-story photo, a real shelf in a real room) with the photo as the hero. If a big offer leads over the photo, it's Offer Banner.
- **Product Animation:** when an offer banner moves (products cycling or popping in under the offer headline), tag both: `product-animation` + `offer-banner`.

## Signals

Big %, $ or struck-through price; "Black Friday Sale", "Lowest price yet", "Best deals of the year"; brand-color or gradient background; product lineup below or beside the offer; small CTA and fine print.
