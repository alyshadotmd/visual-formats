# FAQ Format — Implementation SOP

## Reference examples (look here first)

To find examples, look in the shared `../../files/` folder for any file whose name contains `vf=faq`.

## What this format is

A static that asks a customer question as the headline and answers it in a short paragraph beside the product. It works because it mirrors how a shopper actually thinks ("wait, what does that term mean?") and answers it instantly, in the brand's own voice, rather than making them dig for it.

Strongest for **claims, terms, or ingredients on the pack that shoppers don't immediately understand, and mid-funnel moments where a specific confusion is blocking the sale.**

The one property to protect: **the question has to sound like a real shopper asking it**, not a brand restating its own feature.

## Inputs

Expect **VOC** (reviews, support tickets, comments) surfacing a real question shoppers ask, and the **messaging angle** or claim the answer needs to clarify. VOC and the angle supply the question and the answer; this SOP governs the layout.

## Anatomy

1. **The headline question:** pulled from real customer language, in quotes if it names a term.
2. **The short answer:** one or two sentences beneath the question, with the payoff phrase highlighted in an accent color.
3. **The product visualization:** the product shown in a way that visualizes the answer.
4. **The calm layout:** generous space, logo small in a corner.

## Build rules (priority order)

1. **Pull the question from real customer language** (reviews, support tickets, comments), ideally about a claim or term on the pack that people don't get.
2. **Write the question exactly how a shopper would ask it,** in quotes if it names a term.
3. **Answer in one or two short sentences.** Highlight the payoff phrase in an accent color.
4. **Show the product in a way that visualizes the answer.**
5. **Keep the layout calm:** lots of space, logo small in a corner.
6. **Specific, real claims. No absolutes or banned phrases.**

## Length caps

| Element | Cap |
|---|---|
| Headline question | ≤1 sentence |
| Answer | 1-2 short sentences |

## Build steps

1. Read the brief: the claim or term to clarify and any available VOC.
2. Pull or write the question in real shopper language.
3. Write the one-or-two-sentence answer, highlighting the payoff phrase.
4. Choose the product shot that visualizes the answer.
5. Lay out calmly: generous space, logo small in a corner.
6. Claims pass (answer is specific and validated).
7. Legibility check at thumb size.
8. Deliver: question, answer copy, product visualization note, claims flags.

## QA checklist

- [ ] The question sounds like a real customer, not a brand
- [ ] The answer reads in a few seconds on a phone
- [ ] The visual backs up the answer
- [ ] Layout stays calm: generous space, small logo
- [ ] Honest claims; no absolutes or banned phrases
