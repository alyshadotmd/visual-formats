# Swatch Picker — Classification Guide

**Stage: Emerging.** Seen in only a few ads so far. Promote to Established (change `stage:` above and drop "Emerging." in the index) once it shows up from 3 different brands.

## Definition

A cursor clicks through color swatches like on a product page, and the product photo swaps to each color.

## The things that must all be true

1. Color or variant swatches are visible, like a product page selector.
2. A cursor or tap moves through them and the product image changes with each pick.
3. The point is showing the range of options.

## Commonly confused with

- **Product grid:** shows every variant at once in tiles. Swatch Picker cycles through them one by one with a selector.
