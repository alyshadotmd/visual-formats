# Annotation — Classification Guide

**Stage: Emerging.** Seen in only a few ads so far. Promote to Established (change `stage:` above and drop "Emerging." in the index) once it shows up from 3 different brands.

## Definition

A printed page of copy being marked up by hand (highlighter circles, underlines) as the video reads it.

## The things that must all be true

1. A printed page or document of copy fills most of the frame.
2. A hand marks it up live (highlighter, pen circles, underlines) to point at the key lines.
3. The markup paces the message, usually in sync with a voiceover reading the page.

## Commonly confused with

- **Post-it:** one short handwritten thought on a sticky note. Annotation marks up a full page of printed copy.
- **Whiteboard:** the writing is drawn on a whiteboard from scratch. Annotation marks up copy that is already printed.
