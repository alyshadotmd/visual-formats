# Product Animation Format — Implementation SOP

## Reference examples (look here first)

To find examples, look in the shared `../../files/` folder for any file whose name contains `vf=product-animation`.

**Study a few first** — notice how clean and smooth the motion reads with no live-action people anywhere in frame.

## What this format is

A **motion-graphics** video where products and text animate on screen (slide in, cycle, pop, a price gets crossed out then marked "Free"), with no live-action people. It works because clean digital motion reads as polished and intentional, and animating one idea per beat lets a short video carry several products or price points without ever feeling cluttered or slow.

Strongest for **multi-SKU offers, price reveals and sales, and any moment where the layout itself (not a person) needs to be the hero.**

The one property to protect: **no live-action people appear on camera.** The moment a person is filmed, it's a different format carrying the motion graphics as an overlay, not Product Animation itself.

## Inputs

Expect a **messaging angle** (the offer or benefit the animation reveals) and the **layout it animates within** (an offer banner, a product grid, or similar). The angle decides what the animated copy says; this SOP governs the motion and pacing.

## Anatomy

1. **The layout:** the static frame the animation lives inside (offer banner, product grid, or similar), chosen before animating.
2. **The animated products:** product shots that slide, cycle, or pop into the layout.
3. **The animated text:** kinetic text beats — a benefit line, a price strike, a reveal.
4. **The final frame:** the offer or CTA, held static on the last beat.

## Build rules (priority order)

1. **Pick the layout first** (offer banner, grid) and animate within it.
2. **Animate one idea per beat:** product in, benefit or price, next product.
3. **Keep it short and loopable;** offer or CTA on the last frame.
4. **Keep the motion smooth and digital**, not frame-by-frame photos (that's Stop Motion).
5. **Specific, real claims; no absolutes or banned phrases.**

## Length caps

| Element | Cap |
|---|---|
| Ideas per beat | 1 |
| Final frame | offer/CTA held static |

## Build steps

1. Read the brief: angle, the layout to animate within.
2. Choose the layout (offer banner, grid, etc.) before animating.
3. Storyboard the beats: one idea (product, benefit, or price) per beat.
4. Animate the products and text with smooth digital motion.
5. Lock the final frame on the offer or CTA.
6. Claims pass (validate any price, stat, or benefit claim shown).
7. Legibility check at thumb size for all animated text.
8. Deliver: layout choice, beat-by-beat storyboard, animated copy, final-frame CTA, claims flags.

## QA checklist
- [ ] Animated products or text carry the video
- [ ] No live-action people on camera
- [ ] Smooth digital motion, not frame-by-frame photos
- [ ] Honest claims; no absolutes or banned phrases
