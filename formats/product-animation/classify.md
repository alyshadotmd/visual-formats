# Product Animation — Classification Guide

(Named by Alysha and promoted from the parking lot, 2026-09-30. Media: video.)

## Definition

A **motion-graphics** video where products and text animate on screen (slide in, cycle, pop, a price gets crossed out then marked "Free"), with no live-action people.

## The things that must all be true

1. **Animated products or text** carry the video.
2. **No live-action people** on camera.
3. **Smooth digital motion**, not frame-by-frame photos.

## Quick test

Is it products and text moving on screen with no people? If yes, Product Animation.

## Commonly confused with

- **AI Animation:** a fully AI-generated narrative with animated characters. Product Animation is product motion graphics.
- **Stop Motion:** products moved frame by frame as still photos; handmade, jerky movement.
- **Pairs with a layout format.** Product Animation describes the motion. When the animated frame is clearly a layout format, tag both: `product-animation` + `offer-banner` (products cycling under an offer headline), or `product-animation` + `product-grid` (an animated grid). Products cycling with no offer as the hero is Product Animation alone.

## Signals

Products sliding or cycling, animated price strikes, kinetic text, no people.
