# Challenge Format — Implementation SOP

## Reference examples (look here first)

To find examples, look in the shared `../../files/` folder for any file whose name contains `vf=challenge`.

**Study a few first** — notice how early the rule is stated and how visibly progress is tracked on screen.

## What this format is

A video built around a **live challenge, contest or stunt** with a clear rule, played out on camera (a pie-eating contest, a timed task, a dare). Often the result is tied to the offer, for example "every second someone takes = another percent off," so the viewer watches to see how the deal ends up. It works because a live contest with real stakes creates genuine suspense, and tying the outcome to the offer turns that suspense into a reason to keep watching until the deal lands.

Strongest for **sale/offer moments that benefit from built-in suspense, and brands with a host or personality who can carry the energy of a live stunt.**

The one property to protect: **the outcome has to be a real payoff the viewer watches unfold**, not a foregone conclusion. If the rule or the ending feels staged rather than played out, the suspense collapses.

## Inputs

Expect a **messaging angle** (often an offer or product reveal tied to the challenge's outcome) and the **challenge concept** itself: the rule, the stakes, and how long it runs. The angle decides what the outcome pays off; this SOP governs how the contest is set up and filmed.

## Anatomy

1. **The rule:** stated at the start, on screen and out loud, so the viewer knows exactly what's being attempted and what's at stake.
2. **The host or commentator:** raises the energy and narrates the attempt as it happens.
3. **Visible progress:** a timer, counter, or the offer visibly ticking up as the challenge plays out.
4. **The outcome:** the payoff — often the discount itself or a reveal of the product.

## Build rules (priority order)

1. **Open on the rule as the hook**, on screen and out loud ("every second = 1% off").
2. **Show progress visibly:** a timer, a counter, the % going up.
3. **Keep a host or commentator** to raise the energy.
4. **End on the outcome and the offer it unlocked**, then the product.
5. **Specific, real claims; the final offer has to match what's actually available.**

## Length caps

| Element | Cap |
|---|---|
| CTA | 1, tied to the outcome |

## Build steps

1. Read the brief: angle, what the challenge is meant to prove or unlock.
2. Define the rule and the stakes; confirm the outcome genuinely ties to the offer.
3. Cast or brief the host/commentator.
4. Film the attempt, tracking visible progress (timer, counter, offer ticking up).
5. Capture the outcome and the reveal.
6. Claims pass (confirm the final offer matches what's actually available; validate any stat).
7. Legibility check for on-screen timer/offer text at thumb size.
8. Deliver: rule/stakes brief, host direction, progress-tracking spec, outcome/CTA, claims flags.

## QA checklist
- [ ] The rule is clear in the opening seconds
- [ ] Progress is visible on screen
- [ ] The ending pays off the challenge and lands the offer
- [ ] Honest claims; the final offer matches what's actually available
