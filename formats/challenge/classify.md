# Challenge — Classification Guide

(Named by Alysha, 2026-09-30, from the BFCM swipe file review. Media: video.)

## Reference examples (look here first)

To find examples, look in the shared `../../files/` folder for any file whose name contains `vf=challenge`.

## Definition

A video built around a **live challenge, contest or stunt** with a clear rule, played out on camera (a pie-eating contest, a timed task, a dare). Often the result is tied to the offer, for example "every second she takes = another percent off," so the viewer watches to see how the deal ends up.

## The things that must all be true

1. **There's a challenge with a rule** that's set up at the start (what has to happen, and what's at stake).
2. **It plays out on camera**, with the viewer watching it unfold.
3. **The outcome is the payoff**, often the discount itself or a reveal of the product.

## Quick test

Is the ad "watch someone try to do X, and see what happens"? If yes, Challenge.

## Commonly confused with

- **Skit:** a scripted scene or joke. A Challenge is a contest with a real rule and outcome.
- **Street Interview:** someone with a mic asks strangers questions. A host with a mic can appear in a Challenge, but the contest is the point.
- **Taste Test:** people try the product and react. A Challenge is a task or contest, not a product reaction.

## Signals

"Contest", "challenge" or a dare in the opening text; a timer or counter on screen; a host with a mic narrating; the discount ticking up as the challenge goes on.
