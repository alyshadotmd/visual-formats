# Point to Screen — Implementation SOP

## What it is

A phone films a real screen (laptop, monitor, tablet, another phone) while a hand points at or clicks through what's on it, usually with someone talking you through it.

## Build rules

1. Film a real screen on a phone, handheld and over the shoulder. A little glare and device edge is what makes it feel real; don't swap in a clean screen recording.
2. Open on the screen already showing the thing worth seeing (the deal, the result, the product page), with the hook said out loud in the first line.
3. Every line of the voiceover gets a matching point or click. The hand is the edit: it tells the viewer where to look.
4. Walk one path start to finish (for a shopping walkthrough: offer, pick products, cart showing the price).
5. Add captions for the voiceover so it works on mute.
6. Close by repeating the hook and the urgency ("while the deal is still live").

## QA checklist

- [ ] Real device on camera, not a screen recording
- [ ] Hand points or clicks on every beat
- [ ] Key numbers (price, discount) are readable on a phone
- [ ] Captions on
- [ ] Honest claims; no absolutes or banned phrases
