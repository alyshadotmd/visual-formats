# Venn Diagram Format — Implementation SOP

## Reference examples (look here first)

They live in the shared `../../files/evergreen/` and `../../files/bfcm/` folders, tagged `vf=venn-diagram`. Study them first.

## What this format is

An ad built on a **Venn diagram**: two, three or more overlapping circles, each labeled with a need, benefit or group, and the product sitting in the overlap where they meet.

## Build rules (priority order)

1. Pick 2-3 needs your buyer wants at once.
2. Label each circle in a few words.
3. Put the product in the overlap.
4. Draw it by hand or keep it clean, but make the overlap obvious.

## QA checklist

- [ ] Overlapping circles are the hero
- [ ] Each circle is labeled (a need, a benefit, a time of day, a type of person)
- [ ] The product sits in or points to the overlap
- [ ] Honest claims; no absolutes or banned phrases
