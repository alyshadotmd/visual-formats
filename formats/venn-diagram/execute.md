# Venn Diagram Format — Implementation SOP

## Reference examples (look here first)

To find examples, look in the shared `../../files/` folder for any file whose name contains `vf=venn-diagram`.

## What this format is

An ad built on a **Venn diagram**: two, three or more overlapping circles, each labeled with a need, benefit or group, and the product sitting in the overlap where they meet.

Strongest for **products that satisfy more than one need or benefit at once**, and "the fit for X, Y and Z" multi-benefit positioning.

The one property to protect: **the circles have to actually overlap, with the product in the shared middle** — separate columns or panels are a different format (Us Vs Them), not this one.

## Inputs

Expect a **messaging angle** naming the 2-3 needs, benefits or groups the product satisfies at once, plus the product itself. The angle gives the circle labels; this SOP governs the diagram layout.

## Anatomy

1. **The circles:** two or more overlapping circles.
2. **The labels:** each circle labeled with a need, benefit, time of day, or type of person.
3. **The overlap:** the product sitting in or pointing to the shared middle.
4. **The surface (optional):** drawn by hand (for example on a whiteboard) or rendered clean.

## Build rules (priority order)

1. Pick 2-3 needs your buyer wants at once.
2. Label each circle in a few words.
3. Put the product in the overlap.
4. Draw it by hand or keep it clean, but make the overlap obvious.
5. **Specific, real claims; no absolutes or banned phrases.**

## Length caps

| Element | Cap |
|---|---|
| Circles | 2-3 |
| Label per circle | ≤4 words |
| Product placement | 1, in or pointing to the overlap |

## Build steps

1. Read the brief: angle, the 2-3 needs or benefits to put in circles.
2. Choose 2-3 overlapping circles and write a short label for each.
3. Place the product in or pointing to the shared overlap.
4. Decide hand-drawn vs clean rendering, and keep the overlap visually obvious.
5. Claims pass.
6. Legibility check at thumb size (or on-screen for video).
7. Deliver: circle count and labels, product placement, style direction (hand-drawn vs clean), claims flags.

## QA checklist

- [ ] Overlapping circles are the hero
- [ ] Each circle is labeled (a need, a benefit, a time of day, a type of person)
- [ ] The product sits in or points to the overlap
- [ ] Honest claims; no absolutes or banned phrases
