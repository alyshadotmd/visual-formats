# Feature Benefit Callout Format — Implementation SOP

## Reference examples (look here first)

They live in the shared `../../files/evergreen/` and `../../files/bfcm/` folders, tagged `vf=feature-benefit-callout`. Study them first.

## What this format is

A static where the product sits with a **list of its features or benefits** laid out around or beside it: icon rows, checkmark pills, short labeled lines. The list sells the product, but **nothing points at a specific part of it**. The benefits are about the whole product, not a part of it.

## Build rules (priority order)

1. Pick 3-5 benefits a buyer cares about, each in 2-5 words.
2. Give each one a simple icon or checkmark so the list scans fast.
3. Keep the product big and clear; the list sits beside or around it.
4. One headline that frames the promise the list proves.
5. No arrows to product parts. If a benefit belongs to a part, use Feature Benefit Pointout instead.

## QA checklist

- [ ] The product is shown, usually as the hero or held in hand
- [ ] Several features or benefits are listed (3 or more), as icons, checkmarks or short lines
- [ ] No arrows or connector lines point at specific parts of the product
- [ ] Honest claims; no absolutes or banned phrases
