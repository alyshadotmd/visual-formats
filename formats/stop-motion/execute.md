# Stop Motion Format — Implementation SOP

## Reference examples (look here first)

To find examples, look in the shared `../../files/` folder for any file whose name contains `vf=stop-motion`.

## What this format is

A **stop-motion** video: products or objects move frame by frame as still photos played in sequence, giving a handmade, slightly jerky movement.

Strongest for **playful, attention-grabbing product demos and feature reveals**, where a handmade, DIY craft feel builds charm and stands out from smooth digital motion.

The one property to protect: **the movement has to stay frame-by-frame and handmade** — smooth digital motion is Product Animation instead.

## Inputs

Expect a **messaging angle** (what the moving objects should demonstrate), the real objects or products to animate, and a fixed backdrop. The angle sets what the motion proves; this SOP governs the shoot.

## Anatomy

1. **The backdrop:** one fixed backdrop used in every frame.
2. **The objects:** real products or objects that hop or move into place.
3. **The sequence:** the frame-by-frame moves telling the demonstration.
4. **The close:** the offer or CTA as a final frame or overlay.

## Build rules (priority order)

1. Plan the moves on one fixed backdrop.
2. Shoot one photo per small move (8-12 per second of video).
3. Add the offer or CTA as a final frame or overlay.
4. **Specific, real claims; no absolutes or banned phrases.**

## Length caps

| Element | Cap |
|---|---|
| Photos per second | 8-12 |
| Backdrops | 1 fixed backdrop |

## Build steps

1. Read the brief: angle, the objects available, the demonstration to show.
2. Plan the sequence of small moves on one fixed backdrop.
3. Shoot one photo per small move (8-12 per second of video).
4. Assemble the frames into the sequence.
5. Add the offer or CTA as a final frame or overlay.
6. Claims pass.
7. Deliver: shot list/move plan, backdrop notes, assembled sequence, CTA frame, claims flags.

## QA checklist

- [ ] Frame-by-frame movement of real objects
- [ ] Handmade feel, not smooth digital motion
- [ ] Honest claims; no absolutes or banned phrases
