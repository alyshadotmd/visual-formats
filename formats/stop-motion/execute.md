# Stop Motion Format — Implementation SOP

## What this format is

A **stop-motion** video: products or objects move frame by frame as still photos played in sequence, giving a handmade, slightly jerky movement.

## Build rules (priority order)

1. Plan the moves on one fixed backdrop.
2. Shoot one photo per small move (8-12 per second of video).
3. Add the offer or CTA as a final frame or overlay.

## QA checklist

- [ ] Frame-by-frame movement of real objects
- [ ] Handmade feel, not smooth digital motion
- [ ] Honest claims; no absolutes or banned phrases
