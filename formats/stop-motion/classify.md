# Stop Motion — Classification Guide

(Named by Alysha and promoted from the parking lot, 2026-09-30. Media: video.)

## Definition

A **stop-motion** video: products or objects move frame by frame as still photos played in sequence, giving a handmade, slightly jerky movement.

## The things that must all be true

1. **Frame-by-frame movement** of real objects.
2. **Handmade feel**, not smooth digital motion.

## Quick test

Do real objects hop or move in little jumps, like a flipbook? If yes, Stop Motion.

## Commonly confused with

- **Product Animation:** smooth digital motion graphics.

## Signals

Objects hopping into place, jerky movement, the same backdrop in every frame.
