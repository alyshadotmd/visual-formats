# Handwritten Format — Implementation SOP

## Reference examples (look here first)

To find examples, look in the shared `../../files/` folder for any file whose name contains `vf=handwritten`.

## What this format is

A real product photo with the whole ad layout drawn by hand around it: headline, benefit, price, proof and asides in pen-style lettering and marks, so it looks scribbled together on purpose instead of designed.

Strongest for **offer reveals, myth-busting quotes, and brands wanting a homemade, un-designed feel** that reads as honest rather than polished.

The one property to protect: **it has to read as hand-done, not a script font set in neat lines.**

## Inputs

Expect a **messaging angle** (the headline or quote to write out), a clean product photo, and the offer/price plus any proof to work into the hand marks. The angle carries the message; this SOP governs the hand-lettered layout.

## Anatomy

1. **The product photo:** one clean shot on a plain light background, with room left around it for writing.
2. **The headline:** the hook in the biggest lettering, boxed or underlined, often a quote the customer has heard.
3. **The hand marks:** circles, cross-outs and underlines doing the layout job a designer would normally do with type and shapes (benefit circled, old price crossed out, key word underlined).
4. **Supporting copy:** 4-6 short pieces of copy total, kept to single short lines.
5. **Optional self-aware aside:** a line that winks at the lo-fi look.

## Build rules (priority order)

1. Start with one clean product shot on a plain light background; leave space around it for writing.
2. Lead with a hook headline in the biggest lettering, boxed or underlined (a quote works well, like a line the customer has heard).
3. Use hand marks as the layout: circle the key benefit, cross out the old price, underline "FREE".
4. Keep each line short; 4 to 6 pieces of copy total.
5. One or two pen colors that tie to the brand; keep the letters readable on a phone.
6. Optional: a self-aware aside that winks at the lo-fi look ("our designer is on leave").
7. **Specific, real claims; no absolutes or banned phrases.**

## Length caps

| Element | Cap |
|---|---|
| Headline | biggest lettering on the page, ≤8 words |
| Supporting copy pieces | 4-6 total |
| Pen colors | 1-2 |

## Build steps

1. Read the brief: angle/headline, product shot, offer/price, proof.
2. Start with one clean product shot on a plain light background; leave space for writing.
3. Write the hook headline in the biggest lettering, boxed or underlined.
4. Add hand marks as the layout (circle the benefit, cross out the price, underline a key word).
5. Add the supporting copy (4-6 pieces total).
6. Add the optional self-aware aside.
7. Claims pass (claims specific and validated; no absolutes/banned phrases).
8. Legibility check at thumb size.
9. Deliver: product shot direction, headline, hand-mark layout, supporting copy, pen color notes, claims flags.

## QA checklist

- [ ] It reads as hand-done, not a script font set in neat lines
- [ ] Headline readable at a glance on a phone
- [ ] Offer and proof are clear even in handwriting
- [ ] Honest claims; no absolutes or banned phrases
