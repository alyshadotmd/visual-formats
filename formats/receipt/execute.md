# Receipt Format — Implementation SOP

## Reference examples (look here first)

To find examples, look in the shared `../../files/` folder for any file whose name contains `vf=receipt`.

## What this format is

A static designed to look like a **paper receipt, ticket or tear-off coupon**: a white slip with dotted tear lines, a barcode, itemized lines or one big deal printed on it, over a brand background (often with cut-out products around it). The "you're holding the deal" object is the device.

Strongest for **offer-led ads, discounts and sales, and "here's your deal" reveals** where a tangible object makes the offer feel real.

The one property to protect: **the deal has to read as printed on a holdable paper-slip object** — strip the slip details and it's just an offer banner.

## Inputs

Expect a **messaging angle or offer** (the deal or claim to print), the slip type (receipt vs coupon), and optionally cut-out product assets for the background. The angle carries the deal; this SOP governs the slip staging.

## Anatomy

1. **The slip:** a receipt (itemized) or coupon (one big deal), white with dotted tear lines.
2. **Slip details:** a barcode, tear lines, itemized prices or strikes.
3. **The offer:** the deal printed as the biggest thing on the slip.
4. **The background:** a brand-color background, optionally with cut-out products around the slip.

## Build rules (priority order)

1. Design the slip: receipt (itemized) or coupon (one big deal).
2. Add tear lines and a barcode.
3. Place it on a brand-color background, optionally with cut-out products.
4. Keep the offer the biggest thing on the slip.
5. **Specific, real claims; no absolutes or banned phrases.**

## Length caps

| Element | Cap |
|---|---|
| Offer line | ≤8 words |
| Itemized lines (if receipt) | 3-6 |
| Slip | 1 hero slip |

## Build steps

1. Read the brief: angle/offer, slip type, any itemized lines or proof.
2. Design the slip (receipt or coupon) with tear lines and a barcode.
3. Print the offer as the biggest thing on the slip.
4. Add itemized lines or strikes if it's a receipt.
5. Place the slip on a brand-color background, optionally with cut-out products.
6. Claims pass (offer and prices accurate).
7. Legibility check at thumb size.
8. Deliver: slip type, offer copy, itemized lines, background direction, claims flags.

## QA checklist

- [ ] A paper-slip object (receipt, ticket or coupon) is the hero
- [ ] Slip details such as a barcode, tear lines or itemized prices
- [ ] The deal is printed on the slip
- [ ] Honest claims; no absolutes or banned phrases
