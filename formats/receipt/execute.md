# Receipt Format — Implementation SOP

## What this format is

A static designed to look like a **paper receipt, ticket or tear-off coupon**: a white slip with dotted tear lines, a barcode, itemized lines or one big deal printed on it, over a brand background (often with cut-out products around it). The "you're holding the deal" object is the device.

## Build rules (priority order)

1. Design the slip: receipt (itemized) or coupon (one big deal).
2. Add tear lines and a barcode.
3. Place it on a brand-color background, optionally with cut-out products.
4. Keep the offer the biggest thing on the slip.

## QA checklist

- [ ] A paper-slip object (receipt, ticket or coupon) is the hero
- [ ] Slip details such as a barcode, tear lines or itemized prices
- [ ] The deal is printed on the slip
- [ ] Honest claims; no absolutes or banned phrases
