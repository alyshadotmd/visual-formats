# Cart Screenshot — Classification Guide

**Stage: Emerging.** Seen in only a few ads so far. Promote to Established (change `stage:` above and drop "Emerging." in the index) once it shows up from 3 different brands.

## Definition

A screenshot of an online shopping cart or checkout page, with the discount showing in the cart totals.

## The things that must all be true

1. The main visual is a cart or checkout page (real or recreated), with line items and totals.
2. The discount or savings is visible in the totals (crossed-out price, discount line, final price).
3. The ad lets the viewer see what they'd actually pay, as proof of the deal.

## Commonly confused with

- **Offer banner:** states the deal in big type. Cart Screenshot shows the deal applied in a real-looking cart.
- **Web search:** a search results page. Cart Screenshot is a store's cart or checkout.
