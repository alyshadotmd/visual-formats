# Cart Screenshot Format — Implementation SOP

## Reference examples (look here first)

To find examples, look in the shared `../../files/` folder for any file whose name contains `vf=cart-screenshot`.

## What this format is

A screenshot of an online shopping cart or checkout page, with the discount showing in the cart totals. It works because it lets the viewer see what they'd actually pay, as proof of the deal, rather than just being told about a discount.

Strongest for **discount- or deal-driven offers (sales events like Black Friday) where showing the final price is more convincing than stating it.**

The one property to protect: **the discount has to show up applied in the cart totals** (crossed-out price, discount line, final price), not just stated in copy on top of the image. A clean, believable cart is what makes the proof read as real.

## Inputs

Expect a **messaging angle** — the deal or discount to prove — plus the real product names and prices to populate the cart with. The angle supplies the offer; this SOP governs the cart mock and its legibility.

## Anatomy

1. **The cart/checkout page:** real or recreated, with line items and totals.
2. **The discount:** visible in the totals (crossed-out price, discount line, final price).
3. **The headline copy (optional):** one line on top naming the deal.
4. **CTA/offer close.**

## Build rules (priority order)
1. **Use a clean, believable cart.** Real product names, real prices, the discount line clearly labeled.
2. **Make the savings the most readable number on screen.**
3. **Add one line of copy on top that names the deal**; let the cart prove it.
4. **Keep it to one message, one offer or CTA.**
5. **Honest claims.** No absolutes or banned phrases.

## Length caps

| Element | Cap |
|---|---|
| Headline copy | 1 line |
| Offer/CTA | 1 |

## Build steps

1. Read the brief: the deal/discount to prove and the products to show in the cart.
2. Build a clean, believable cart or checkout mock with real product names and prices.
3. Apply the discount so it's visible in the totals (crossed-out price, discount line, final price).
4. Add one line of copy on top naming the deal.
5. Claims pass: discount and prices specific and validated, no absolutes or banned phrases.
6. Legibility check: confirm it reads at a glance on a phone.
7. Deliver: cart mock, headline copy, offer/CTA, claims flags.

## QA checklist
- [ ] Cart or checkout page is the hero, with line items and totals
- [ ] Discount or savings is visible in the totals (crossed-out price, discount line, final price)
- [ ] The device carries the message
- [ ] It reads at a glance on a phone
- [ ] Honest claims; no absolutes or banned phrases
