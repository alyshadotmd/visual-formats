# Cart Screenshot — Implementation SOP

## What it is

A screenshot of an online shopping cart or checkout page, with the discount showing in the cart totals.

## Build rules

1. Use a clean, believable cart: real product names, real prices, the discount line clearly labeled.
2. Make the savings the most readable number on screen.
3. Add one line of copy on top that names the deal; let the cart prove it.
4. One message, one offer or CTA.

## QA checklist

- [ ] The device carries the message
- [ ] It reads at a glance on a phone
- [ ] Honest claims; no absolutes or banned phrases
