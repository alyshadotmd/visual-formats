# Shelfie Format — Implementation SOP

## Reference examples (look here first)

To find examples, look in the shared `../../files/` folder for any file whose name contains `vf=shelfie`.

## What this format is

A styled shot of the products lined up on a shelf, borrowing the "shelfie" people post of their own bathroom or vanity. It works because it shows the products as something you'd be proud to display, and it shows the range at once.

Strongest for **beauty, skincare, haircare, fragrance, home and any range meant to be displayed**, plus "award winners" or "bestsellers" moments.

The one property to protect: **it must look like a real, desirable shelf**, not a catalog cut-out.

## Inputs

Expect a **messaging angle** and the products to feature (usually 3-6).

## Anatomy

1. **The shelf:** one or two tiers, a real-looking material (marble, glass, wood).
2. **The products:** upright, spaced evenly, labels facing out.
3. **The setting:** matching tones and soft or moody light.
4. **Short copy on top:** headline or offer ("25% Off Award Winners").
5. **Brand and optional CTA.**

## Build rules (priority order)
1. **Shoot front-on or slightly angled** so the shelf edge reads clearly.
2. **Style for display.** Even spacing, labels forward, tones that match the brand.
3. **Light for mood.** Soft, directional light; avoid flat catalog lighting.
4. **Keep copy short and in the open space** above or between tiers.
5. **Real products, honest claims.** No absolutes or banned phrases.

## Length caps

| Element | Cap |
|---|---|
| Headline / offer | ≤6 words |
| CTA | 0-1 |

## Build steps

1. Read the brief: angle and products.
2. Choose shelf material and tones.
3. Arrange products upright, labels forward, evenly spaced.
4. Light for mood; shoot front-on.
5. Add headline, brand, CTA.
6. Claims pass; thumb-size check.
7. Deliver: shelf and styling direction, product order, copy, CTA.

## QA checklist
- [ ] Clearly a shelf scene, shot front-on
- [ ] Products upright, labels forward, evenly spaced
- [ ] Aesthetic, display-worthy lighting and tones
- [ ] Short copy; honest; no absolutes/banned phrases
