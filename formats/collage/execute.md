# Collage Format — Implementation SOP

## Reference examples (look here first)

To find examples, look in the shared `../../files/` folder for any file whose name contains `vf=collage`.

## What this format is

A collage that imitates the picks pages creators post to Pinterest and Instagram stories. It works because it looks like curation, not advertising: people save and share these picks pages, and the mix of accessories and styling sells a mood and a persona, not only a product.

Strongest for **gifting moments (BFCM, holiday, Mother's Day, Valentine's), multi-product ranges, and aesthetic / lifestyle brands.**

The one property to protect: **it has to feel hand-curated and shareable.** If it reads as a tidy brand layout, it's a bento grid or product grid instead.

## Inputs

Expect a **messaging angle** (who the picks are for, the moment), the products to feature, and a mood for the props.

## Anatomy

1. **Curated-picks title:** big and bold ("Black Friday Sale Picks", "Gifts for the girl who has everything").
2. **Cut-out products:** 3-7 of the brand's products, clipped clean.
3. **Mood props and people:** accessories, styling, a person from behind, chosen for the persona.
4. **Plain background:** white, cream or one soft solid color.
5. **Small shop button or line:** optional ("Shop Black Friday").

## Build rules (priority order)
1. **Scatter, don't grid.** Vary sizes, let items overlap slightly, keep it balanced but loose.
2. **Mix in non-product items** that tell the viewer who this is for.
3. **Keep the title as the only big text.** Offer copy is optional; the picks are the point.
4. **One plain background color** so the cut-outs pop.
5. **Real products, honest claims.** No absolutes or banned phrases.

## Length caps

| Element | Cap |
|---|---|
| Title | ≤6 words |
| Supporting line | ≤6 words (optional) |
| CTA | 0-1 small button |

## Build steps

1. Read the brief: moment, persona, products.
2. Pick the props and people that signal the persona.
3. Clip everything and compose a loose, balanced collage on one plain color.
4. Add the title and optional button.
5. Claims pass.
6. Thumb-size check: title legible, products recognizable.
7. Deliver: item list, background color, title, CTA.

## QA checklist
- [ ] Reads like a creator's picks page, not a brand grid
- [ ] Loose, overlapping cut-outs; no boxed tiles
- [ ] Non-product mood items included
- [ ] Title is the only big text; honest; no absolutes/banned phrases
