# UGC Format — Implementation SOP

## What this format is

A **fallback** tag for user-generated-style content: a creator-style video that is clearly UGC but doesn't match any specific UGC format in this library. Specific UGC formats (`yapper`, `testimonial`, `interview`, `street-interview`, `ugc-mashup`, `unboxing`, `taste-test` and the rest) always win when they fit.

## Build rules (priority order)

1. Cast a real-seeming creator in their own space.
2. Show the product in use in their day.
3. Keep the edit native to social (phone footage, simple cuts, captions).
4. If the video becomes a clear specific format (a yap, a testimonial arc), brief it as that format instead.

## QA checklist

- [ ] It looks like creator content, not a brand production: a real-seeming person, phone-camera feel, their own space
- [ ] A person is on camera for a meaningful part of it (talking, using the product)
- [ ] No specific UGC format fits cleanly
- [ ] Honest claims; no absolutes or banned phrases
