# UGC — Classification Guide

(Named by Alysha and promoted from the parking lot, 2026-09-30. Media: video.)

## Reference examples (look here first)

To find examples, look in the shared `../../files/` folder for any file whose name contains `vf=ugc`.

## Definition

A **fallback** tag for user-generated-style content: a creator-style video that is clearly UGC but doesn't match any specific UGC format in this library. Specific UGC formats (`yapper`, `testimonial`, `interview`, `street-interview`, `ugc-mashup`, `unboxing`, `taste-test` and the rest) always win when they fit.

## The things that must all be true

1. **It looks like creator content**, not a brand production: a real-seeming person, phone-camera feel, their own space.
2. **A person is on camera** for a meaningful part of it (talking, using the product). All b-roll with text and no person talking is `b-roll-overlay`.
3. **No specific UGC format fits cleanly.** Check the specific ones first.

## Quick test

Is it creator content, and have you ruled out every specific UGC format? If yes, UGC.

## Commonly confused with

- **Yapper:** raw, direct to camera, little to no editing. A polished creator video with b-roll or voiceover is not a yapper; if nothing more specific fits, it's UGC (Alysha, 2026-09-30).
- **Testimonial:** one person telling their own story with the product as a clear arc. Use it when that arc is there.
- **UGC Mashup:** several creators cut together. UGC is one creator.
- **B-roll Overlay:** no one on camera talking.

## Signals

Phone camera, creator's home, kitchen or workplace, product used on screen, some editing and b-roll.
