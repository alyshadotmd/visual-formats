# Transformation — Classification Guide

(Named by Alysha and promoted from the parking lot, 2026-09-30. Media: video.)

## Definition

A video that tells a **problem-to-result story in sequence**: it opens on the problem (hair in the drain, breakouts, clutter), brings in the product, and ends on the happy result. Unlike before-and-after, the before and after are not matched shots of the same thing.

## The things that must all be true

1. **Opens on a problem.**
2. **Brings in the product.**
3. **Ends on the result**, as a story rather than a matched comparison.

## Quick test

Is it a short journey from pain to payoff, without a matched before-and-after shot? If yes, Transformation.

## Commonly confused with

- **Before-and-After:** a direct, matched comparison of the same subject (before hair vs after hair).
- **Testimonial:** someone narrates their whole story on camera.

## Signals

Problem shot, product shot, result shot; story-style text beats.
