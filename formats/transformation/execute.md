# Transformation Format — Implementation SOP

## What this format is

A video that tells a **problem-to-result story in sequence**: it opens on the problem (hair in the drain, breakouts, clutter), brings in the product, and ends on the happy result. Unlike before-and-after, the before and after are not matched shots of the same thing.

## Build rules (priority order)

1. Show the problem vividly in the first beat.
2. Introduce the product as the turn.
3. End on the result and the offer.

## QA checklist

- [ ] Opens on a problem
- [ ] Brings in the product
- [ ] Ends on the result, as a story rather than a matched comparison
- [ ] Honest claims; no absolutes or banned phrases
