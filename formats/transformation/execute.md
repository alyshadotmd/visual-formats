# Transformation Format — Implementation SOP

## Reference examples (look here first)

To find examples, look in the shared `../../files/` folder for any file whose name contains `vf=transformation`.

## What this format is

A video that tells a **problem-to-result story in sequence**: it opens on the problem (hair in the drain, breakouts, clutter), brings in the product, and ends on the happy result. Unlike before-and-after, the before and after are not matched shots of the same thing.

Strongest for **problem-aware audiences and products whose results unfold over a routine or process** (hair, skin, home, habits), where a short story arc lands better than a single matched comparison shot.

The one property to protect: **the problem and result have to feel like a sequence, a story**, not a matched comparison shot — a matched before/after belongs to Before-and-After instead.

## Inputs

Expect a **messaging angle** (the problem and the payoff), a problem scene or asset, and a result scene or asset. The angle sets the promise; this SOP governs the story sequence.

## Anatomy

1. **The problem beat:** opens vividly on the problem.
2. **The product beat:** the product introduced as the turn.
3. **The result beat:** ends on the happy result.
4. **Story-style text (optional):** on-screen text beats reinforcing the arc.

## Build rules (priority order)

1. Show the problem vividly in the first beat.
2. Introduce the product as the turn.
3. End on the result and the offer.
4. **Specific, real claims; no absolutes or banned phrases.**

## Length caps

| Element | Cap |
|---|---|
| Problem beat | first third of the runtime |
| Result beat | final third of the runtime |

## Build steps

1. Read the brief: angle, the problem, the product's role, the result.
2. Open on the problem vividly.
3. Introduce the product as the turn.
4. End on the result and the offer.
5. Add story-style text beats if useful.
6. Claims pass (result honest and specific).
7. Deliver: problem beat, product beat, result beat, text-beat notes, claims flags.

## QA checklist

- [ ] Opens on a problem
- [ ] Brings in the product
- [ ] Ends on the result, as a story rather than a matched comparison
- [ ] Honest claims; no absolutes or banned phrases
