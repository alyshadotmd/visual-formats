# News Format — Implementation SOP

## Reference examples (look here first)

To find examples, look in the shared `../../files/` folder for any file whose name contains `vf=news`.

## What this format is

An ad **made to look like a news broadcast or news alert**: a lower-third banner, a "BREAKING" or "NEWS FLASH" tag, headline-style copy, sometimes an anchor or news set. The product is framed as the news or the answer to it. It works because the broadcast framing borrows the urgency and authority of real news, so the viewer reads the claim as a reported fact before they read it as an ad.

Strongest for **problem-aware hooks that can be framed as a trend, a study, or a sudden shift worth reporting on**, and categories where "people are switching to X" or "this just changed" lands as news.

The one property to protect: **the news styling has to be the hero** and the copy has to read as a headline, not as ad copy.

## Inputs

Expect a **messaging angle** that can be framed as a headline (a trend, a study, a problem worth "reporting"), and a striking image of the problem the product answers. The angle supplies the story; this SOP governs the broadcast staging.

## Anatomy

1. **The news tag or banner:** a "BREAKING" or "NEWS FLASH" tag, a lower-third banner, or a ticker that reads as news instantly.
2. **The headline:** the angle written as a news headline, not as ad copy.
3. **The problem image:** a striking photo or clip of the problem the headline is reporting on.
4. **The product as the answer:** the product lands as the resolution to the story, sometimes with an anchor or news-set look.

## Build rules (priority order)

1. **Write the hook as a headline** ("BREAKING: ...") rather than as ad copy.
2. **Use a news tag, banner, or ticker** so it reads as news instantly.
3. **Pair it with a striking photo of the problem.**
4. **Land the product as the answer** to the story.
5. **Specific, real claims. No absolutes or banned phrases.**

## Length caps

| Element | Cap |
|---|---|
| Headline | ≤12 words |
| News tag/ticker text | Short, scannable at thumb size |

## Build steps

1. Read the brief: the angle's story and the problem it reports on.
2. Write the headline as breaking news, not as ad copy.
3. Choose the news tag, banner, or ticker style.
4. Pair the headline with a striking image or clip of the problem.
5. Land the product as the answer to the story.
6. Claims pass (headline and any stats are real and specific).
7. Legibility check at thumb size.
8. Deliver: headline, tag/banner style, problem imagery, product resolution, claims flags.

## QA checklist

- [ ] News styling is the hero: a breaking-news tag, news banner, ticker, anchor or news set
- [ ] The copy reads like a headline, not like ad copy
- [ ] The product is tied to the story (the news it answers, or the news itself)
- [ ] Legible at thumb size
- [ ] Honest claims; no absolutes or banned phrases
