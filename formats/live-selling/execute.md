# Live Selling Format — Implementation SOP

## Reference examples (look here first)

To find examples, look in the shared `../../files/` folder for any file whose name contains `vf=live-selling`.

## What this format is

A video that mimics a live-selling or live-shopping stream: one or more presenters talking to camera as if live, with on-screen comment or review bubbles, product call-outs, and a deal pitched in real time. It works because the live-stream cues (comment bubbles, a live badge, viewer reactions) create a sense that other people are watching and buying right now, which makes the deal feel more real and time-bound.

Strongest for **deal- and urgency-driven offers, multi-product pitches, and drops or sales events** where real-time social proof sharpens the pitch.

The one property to protect: **it has to feel like a live stream caught in the moment.** The comment bubbles, live badge, and real-time pacing are what sell it; drop those and it's just a presenter talking (`yapper`).

## Inputs

Expect a **messaging angle** — the products to pitch and the deal or urgency to land — plus the number of hosts and the products to walk through. The angle supplies the pitch and offer; this SOP governs the live-stream staging.

## Anatomy

1. **The set:** a live-stream-style set with 1-2 hosts.
2. **The hosts:** presenters pitching to camera as if live.
3. **Live-stream cues:** on-screen comment or review bubbles, a live badge, or viewer reactions.
4. **Product call-outs:** products walked through and shown.
5. **The deal:** pitched in real time with urgency.

### Beats (in order)
1. Hosts open as if live, establishing the stream feel.
2. Comment or review bubbles overlay as they talk, building social proof.
3. Walk through the products one by one with call-outs.
4. Land the deal with urgency to close.

## Build rules (priority order)
1. **Stage a live-stream set with 1-2 hosts.**
2. **Overlay comment or review bubbles as they talk.**
3. **Walk through the products and land the deal with urgency.**
4. **Keep live-stream cues visible throughout** (live badge, viewer reactions) so it reads as live, not a scripted pitch.
5. **Honest claims.** No absolutes or banned phrases.

## Length caps

| Element | Cap |
|---|---|
| Hosts | 1-2 |
| Live-stream cues | present throughout (bubbles/badge/reactions) |

## Build steps

1. Read the brief: the products to pitch and the deal/urgency to land.
2. Stage the live-stream set with 1-2 hosts.
3. Shoot the pitch as if live, presenters talking directly to camera.
4. Overlay comment or review bubbles as they talk.
5. Walk through the products with call-outs.
6. Land the deal with urgency.
7. Claims pass: claims specific and validated, no absolutes or banned phrases.
8. Deliver: host/set note, product walkthrough order, bubble/overlay copy, deal copy, claims flags.

## QA checklist
- [ ] Presenters pitch to camera as if live
- [ ] Live-stream cues such as comment or review bubbles, a live badge or viewer reactions
- [ ] Products and a deal pitched in real time
- [ ] Honest claims; no absolutes or banned phrases
