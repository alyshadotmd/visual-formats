# Founder Format — Visual Execution SOP

## Reference examples (look here first)

- **Reference examples:** They live in the shared `../../files/` folder, tagged `vf=founder`.

**Whenever this SOP is triggered or referenced, open the shared `../../files/` folder (filter for `vf=founder`) and watch the examples first** — how much polish, what the setting communicates, and how the founder holds the camera's attention without performing.

## What this format is, visually

The founder, on camera, talking. **It is always a video.** It leans on authority and motive over production polish, but a studio or polished setup is completely fine, the credibility comes from who is speaking and what only they can reveal, not the setting. **This visual identity is constant no matter which spine the script uses or whether the founder is fronting a standalone Founder ad or guest-starring inside another format** (see the script SOP for that layering rule); the way it's shot doesn't change based on the argument.

## Setting

Flexible either direction: their real space (office, workshop, kitchen table, wherever the work actually happened) or a clean studio setup both work. Match the setting to what serves the story, a workshop reads as evidence for a product-development reveal; a studio reads as considered and confident for a belief/manifesto spine. Setting is a supporting signal, never a requirement.

## Camera and performance

- Eye-line to camera, direct, not off-axis to an interviewer.
- Minimal cuts. This format earns trust through sustained, unbroken attention, not editing energy.
- Performance should read like the founder explaining something to a friend, not presenting to a room. If it looks rehearsed, it undercuts the format's entire premise.
- B-roll only where it supports the story (the workshop, the early prototype, the notebook), never as filler or to break up a slow section, if the section is slow, that's a script problem, not an edit problem.

## Production handoff

Captions on. Note b-roll needs against specific story beats (the founder describing a prototype should have a shot of it, if one exists). Runtime target comes from the script (20-45s standard, up to 60s for a considered/high-AOV case), production shouldn't pad or cut it independently of what the script needs.

## QA checklist (visual/production only)

- [ ] It's a video of the founder speaking (never a static)
- [ ] Eye-line direct to camera, minimal cuts
- [ ] Setting matches the chosen spine (workshop for development reveals, studio fine for belief/manifesto)
- [ ] Performance reads like explaining to a friend, not a rehearsed read
- [ ] B-roll (if any) tied to specific story beats, not generic filler
- [ ] Captions on
