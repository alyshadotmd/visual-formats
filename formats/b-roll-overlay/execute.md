# B-roll Overlay Format — Implementation SOP

## Reference examples (look here first)

To find examples, look in the shared `../../files/` folder for any file whose name contains `vf=b-roll-overlay`.

## What this format is

A video made only of **b-roll footage** (product, hands, lifestyle shots) with **text overlaid** to carry the message. No voiceover and no one talking on camera. It works because the footage sets a calm, aesthetic mood while the on-screen text does all the selling, so the ad reads as polished content rather than a pitch delivered at the viewer.

Strongest for **aesthetic, lifestyle-led brands and moments where a quiet, cinematic feel should carry the product** rather than a spoken pitch.

The one property to protect: **no voiceover and no one talking to camera** — the moment a person speaks on camera, it stops being this format.

## Inputs

Expect a **messaging angle** broken into short on-screen lines, and the **b-roll beats** available (product, hands, lifestyle shots) to pair with them. The angle supplies the line-by-line story; this SOP governs the footage pacing and overlay.

## Anatomy

1. **The b-roll beats:** 5-10 shots of product, hands, or lifestyle moments, built around one action or story.
2. **The text overlay:** short on-screen lines that tell the story in order, timed to the cuts.
3. **The music bed:** music only, no voiceover.
4. **The close:** ends on the offer or CTA.

## Build rules (priority order)

1. **Shoot 5-10 b-roll beats** around one action or story.
2. **Write short on-screen lines** that tell the story in order.
3. **Music, no voiceover.**
4. **No one talks to camera** at any point.
5. **End on the offer or CTA.**
6. **Specific, real claims. No absolutes or banned phrases.**

## Length caps

| Element | Cap |
|---|---|
| Number of b-roll beats | 5-10 |
| Each on-screen line | ≤8 words |

## Build steps

1. Read the brief: the angle's story broken into beats.
2. Shoot or select 5-10 b-roll beats around that story.
3. Write short on-screen lines, timed to the cuts, telling the story in order.
4. Add a music bed; confirm no voiceover and no one on camera talking.
5. End on the offer or CTA.
6. Claims pass (on-screen claims are specific and validated).
7. Legibility / sound-off check: the story reads fully with sound off.
8. Deliver: shot list, on-screen line copy, music direction, CTA, claims flags.

## QA checklist

- [ ] Only b-roll footage
- [ ] Text overlay carries the message
- [ ] No voiceover and no one talking to camera
- [ ] The story reads fully sound-off
- [ ] Honest claims; no absolutes or banned phrases
