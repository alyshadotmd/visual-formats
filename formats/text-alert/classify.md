# Text Alert — Classification Guide

(Named by Alysha and promoted from the parking lot, 2026-09-30. Media: video.)

## Definition

A video where a **phone notification** (an iMessage or app banner) drops into an everyday moment; the person taps it and the ad cuts to the product and the offer.

## The things that must all be true

1. **A notification banner** appears on screen.
2. **It interrupts a moment** (someone mid-task, or a phone lock screen).
3. **It leads into the product or offer.**

## Quick test

Is a phone notification the hook that leads to the offer? If yes, Text Alert.

## Commonly confused with

- **Text Message:** a whole conversation thread. Text Alert is one banner as the hook.
- **Notes App:** a notes page.

## Signals

iMessage or app banner at the top of the screen, lock screen, tap, cut to product.
