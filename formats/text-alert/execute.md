# Text Alert Format — Implementation SOP

## What this format is

A video where a **phone notification** (an iMessage or app banner) drops into an everyday moment; the person taps it and the ad cuts to the product and the offer.

## Build rules (priority order)

1. Open on an everyday moment or lock screen.
2. Drop in a banner with the hook line.
3. Tap, then cut to the product and offer.

## QA checklist

- [ ] A notification banner appears on screen
- [ ] It interrupts a moment (someone mid-task, or a phone lock screen)
- [ ] It leads into the product or offer
- [ ] Honest claims; no absolutes or banned phrases
