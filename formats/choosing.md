# Visual formats — choosing a format

Format is the container for the offer. Match the container's purpose to the stage's job and to the trigger moment: formats that reveal for stages that reveal a problem, formats that prove for stages that prove superiority, formats that can stage a scene when the trigger moment is the ad. Any format can work at any stage when the messaging is right; some just live more naturally at one stage than another.

1. **Start from the brand's own results.** A format that already wins for this message goes first.
2. **Match how it's made and the stage.** Top = unaware and problem-aware, middle = solution- and product-aware, bottom = most aware.
3. **Check the inputs.** Keep the formats the brand has the material for; name the one thing that would unlock a better one.
4. **Read "Strongest for" against the hook and the product**, keep two or three, then open each one's `execute.md` and examples.

**Stage source:** `Alysha` = placed by Alysha (TOF, MOF or BOF planning, Oct 2026); `guide` = the format's own "Strongest for" line; `proposed` = suggested from the format's job, for Alysha to confirm or change.
