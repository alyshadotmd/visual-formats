# UGC Mashup — Classification Guide

(Named by Alysha and promoted from the parking lot, 2026-09-30. Media: video.)

## Definition

A UGC video cut together from **several different creators'** clips into one ad, where no single clip carries a specific format on its own.

## The things that must all be true

1. **Two or more different creators** appear.
2. **The clips are cut together** into one piece (quick hand-offs between people).
3. **Creator-style footage**, not brand production.

## Quick test

Do you see more than one creator, with their clips spliced into one ad? If yes, UGC Mashup.

## Commonly confused with

- **UGC:** one creator.
- **Testimonial:** one person's story arc.
- **Interview / Street Interview:** people answering an interviewer's questions.

## Signals

Rapid cuts between different faces and homes, matching captions across clips, often one shared message or offer at the end.
