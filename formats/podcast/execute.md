# Podcast Format — Implementation SOP

## Reference examples (look here first)

They live in the shared `../../files/evergreen/` and `../../files/bfcm/` folders, tagged `vf=podcast`. Study them first.

## What this format is

A video built from **clips made to look like a podcast recording**: people talking at podcast microphones, headphones, a podcast set or couch, often with a host and guest. The product comes up in the conversation like it would on a real show.

## Build rules (priority order)

1. Set the scene: podcast mics, headphones, a lived-in podcast set.
2. Open on a real-sounding moment from the conversation.
3. Let the product come up naturally in the talk.
4. Cut to product and offer, or keep the whole ad on the set.

## QA checklist

- [ ] A podcast setup is visible: a podcast microphone on an arm or stand, headphones, a podcast-style set
- [ ] It plays as a clip from a conversation, not a talk straight to the viewer
- [ ] The podcast clip is a real part of the ad, often the hook
- [ ] Honest claims; no absolutes or banned phrases
