# Found Footage Format — Implementation SOP

## Reference examples (look here first)

To find examples, look in the shared `../../files/` folder for any file whose name contains `vf=found-footage`.

## What this format is

The ad plays out through a device camera's view, so the viewer feels like they caught something real. It works because people trust footage nobody seems to have staged, and because the device look (fisheye, timestamp, LIVE badge) is instantly recognizable in a feed.

Strongest for **home/security products, baby and pet monitors, and "caught on camera" surprise or reveal moments** where the device itself can plausibly be the hero.

The one property to protect: **it has to read as real device footage in the first second.**

## Inputs

Expect a **messaging angle**, the device that fits the story (doorbell, dashcam, baby monitor, pet cam, security cam), and the surprising or funny moment the device catches. The angle carries the message; this SOP governs the device staging.

## Anatomy

1. **The device view:** the camera look matched to the device, its angle, lens, overlay (timestamp, LIVE, battery, app buttons), and quality (grain, night vision).
2. **The caught moment:** the surprising or funny thing the device catches, opening the footage.
3. **The message beat:** the message landing inside the footage itself, through a person talking to the camera, sticky notes, or a reaction.
4. **The product cut:** the cut to the product and offer once the footage has made its point.

## Build rules (priority order)

1. **Pick the device that fits the story:** doorbell for deliveries, baby monitor for parents, dashcam for the commute, pet cam for pets, security cam for "caught in the act."
2. **Match the device look:** its angle and lens, its overlay (timestamp, LIVE, battery, app buttons), its quality.
3. **Open on the moment**, something surprising or funny caught on camera.
4. **Let the message land inside the footage** (a person talking to the camera, sticky notes, a reaction), then cut to the product and offer.
5. **Keep it short**; this format works on a quick reveal.
6. **Specific, real claims; no absolutes or banned phrases.**

## Length caps

| Element | Cap |
|---|---|
| Device-view footage | majority of the runtime, not a one-second cutaway |
| Message beat | 1 clear moment |
| Product cut | final few seconds |

## Build steps

1. Read the brief: angle, the device, the moment it catches.
2. Pick the device that fits the story.
3. Match the device look (angle, overlay, quality).
4. Open on the moment.
5. Let the message land inside the footage.
6. Cut to the product and offer.
7. Claims pass (specific, honest; validate any numbers).
8. Deliver: device choice, overlay/look notes, moment script, product cut, claims flags.

## QA checklist

- [ ] Reads as device footage in the first second
- [ ] Device angle and overlay are believable
- [ ] The message lands inside the footage
- [ ] Honest claims; no absolutes or banned phrases
