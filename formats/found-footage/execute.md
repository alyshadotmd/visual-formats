# Found Footage Format — Implementation SOP

## What this format is

The ad plays out through a device camera's view, so the viewer feels like they caught something real. It works
because people trust footage nobody seems to have staged, and because the device look (fisheye, timestamp, LIVE badge)
is instantly recognizable in a feed.

The one property to protect: **it has to read as real device footage in the first second.**

## Build rules (priority order)

1. **Pick the device that fits the story:** doorbell for deliveries, baby monitor for parents, dashcam for the commute,
   pet cam for pets, security cam for "caught in the act."
2. **Match the device look:** its angle and lens, its overlay (timestamp, LIVE, battery, app buttons), its quality.
3. **Open on the moment**, something surprising or funny caught on camera.
4. **Let the message land inside the footage** (a person talking to the camera, sticky notes, a reaction), then cut to
   the product and offer.
5. **Keep it short**; this format works on a quick reveal.

## QA checklist

- [ ] Reads as device footage in the first second
- [ ] Device angle and overlay are believable
- [ ] The message lands inside the footage
- [ ] Honest claims; no absolutes or banned phrases
