# Split Screen Format — Implementation SOP

## Reference examples (look here first)

To find examples, look in the shared `../../files/` folder for any file whose name contains `vf=split-screen`.

**Study a few first** — notice how the fixed panel stays simple and readable while the live panel carries the proof or energy.

## What this format is

The frame is split into two side-by-side panels that run at the same time for most of the ad. Usually one panel is fixed and designed (logo, product shot, the offer) and the other plays live footage (a creator talking, product swatches, a demo). It works because the viewer gets the pitch and the proof at once — the offer never has to compete for screen time with the thing proving it's worth taking.

Strongest for **sale/offer moments paired with social proof or demo footage**, where the discount needs to stay visible the whole time the proof plays.

The one property to protect: **both panels stay on screen together for the whole ad.** If the frame cuts from one shot to another instead of holding both at once, it's not Split Screen.

## Inputs

Expect a **messaging angle** (what the fixed panel should say: offer, product, logo) and the **live footage** for the other panel (a creator, a demo, product close-ups). The angle sets the fixed panel's message; this SOP governs how the two panels sit together.

## Anatomy

1. **The fixed panel:** a designed, static panel carrying the logo, product shot, or offer (sale name, % off, CTA).
2. **The live panel:** moving footage running alongside it — a creator talking, swatches, the product in use.
3. **The split itself:** the two panels share the frame (side by side or top and bottom) for the whole ad, each doing a different job.

## Build rules (priority order)

1. **Keep the offer panel fixed and simple:** logo, sale name, the % off, a CTA.
2. **Put the moving proof in the other panel:** a creator talking, swatches, the product in use.
3. **Keep both panels on screen the whole time;** don't cut away to full frame.
4. **Make sure the live panel still reads on mute** (captions on any talking).
5. **Specific, real claims; no absolutes or banned phrases.**

## Length caps

| Element | Cap |
|---|---|
| Panels | exactly 2, on screen together for the whole ad |
| Fixed-panel text | logo + sale name + % off + 1 CTA |
| Captions | on for any talking in the live panel |

## Build steps

1. Read the brief: angle for the fixed panel, footage available for the live panel.
2. Design the fixed panel: logo, sale name, % off, CTA.
3. Select or shoot the live footage for the other panel.
4. Lock the split layout (side by side or top and bottom) and hold both panels for the full runtime.
5. Add captions to any talking in the live panel.
6. Claims pass (validate the offer and any claim in the live footage).
7. Legibility check at thumb size for the fixed panel.
8. Deliver: fixed-panel copy/design, live-footage direction, split layout note, caption direction, claims flags.

## QA checklist
- [ ] Two panels are visible together for most of the ad
- [ ] One panel carries the offer or product, the other carries live footage
- [ ] The offer is readable at a glance
- [ ] Honest claims; no absolutes or banned phrases
