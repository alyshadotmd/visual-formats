# Interview — Classification Guide

(Named by Alysha and promoted from the parking lot, 2026-09-30. Media: video.)

## Reference examples (look here first)

To find examples, look in the shared `../../files/` folder for any file whose name contains `vf=interview`.

## Definition

A video built around **interviewing people about the product**: an interviewer asks questions and one or more people answer on camera about their experience with it.

## The things that must all be true

1. **An interviewer's questions** drive it (heard, seen or shown as text).
2. **People answer on camera** about the product.
3. **A planned setting**, not strangers stopped in public.

## Quick test

Is someone being interviewed about the product? If yes, Interview (or Street Interview if strangers are stopped in public).

## Commonly confused with

- **Street Interview:** strangers stopped in public.
- **Testimonial:** a person tells their own story with no interviewer.
- **UGC Mashup:** several creators cut together, no interviewer.

## Signals

Question cards, a mic or interviewer off camera, answers to camera, sometimes several interviewees.
