## Pre-analysis (creative analysis skill)

**Step 0: the ad's job**
- Entry point: desire. A small everyday bag that still fits everything, looks cute, and lasts.
- Persona: someone shopping for an everyday crossbody who is stuck on the size question and has been burned by bags that peel or wear out.
- Awareness: product-aware. The ad answers a buying question (which size?) and handles objections. But the scene teaches the product from zero, so a cold viewer can still follow it.
- Funnel job: full-sell, done softly. It covers size, how to wear it, quality, colors, and a link.
- Distribution: built to look like an organic TikTok reply. Works whitelisted/partnership and cold in-feed because the comment bubble gives the context.

**Hook**
- Spoken: "This is the small, it literally fits everything I need."
- Visual: the bag held open with its contents showing.
- Text: the comment bubble "What size did you get?"
- Tactic: comment response (primary) with a bold claim ("fits everything").
- Triggers: social proof (a real person asked), identity call-out (anyone with the same question).
- Alignment: fully aligned. She says it fits everything while you see it fit everything. Proof in the first frame.

**Mechanic**
- The borrowed question: the objection comes from a real commenter, so the answer reads as help, not a pitch. Layered with a quiet borrowed enemy: "not the kind of leather that's gonna peel or crack" points at cheap faux leather without naming anyone.

**Format**
- Comment Response + Yapper (single creator, direct to camera, light cuts).

**Body**
- Structure is one objection after another, in the order a buyer thinks: size, then how to wear it, then quality, then colors.
- "I'm really hard on bags" is a confession that makes the durability claim believable.
- CTA is friend language: "I'll put the link right down there."

**What's working**
1. The question is the hook. Nobody has to be convinced the topic matters.
2. Proof shows up in the first second, before the claim even finishes.
3. Brand name lands at 0:07, after the viewer is already watching for the answer.
4. Every few seconds answers the next question, so there's always a reason to keep watching.
5. The durability claim is carried by a personal admission, not a brand promise.

**What could be stronger**
1. Sound-off viewers lose almost everything after the comment bubble disappears at 0:02. No captions, no persistent headline.
2. "Link right down there" is organic TikTok language. On Meta it still feels native, but it points at a link that isn't where she's pointing.

**Highest-leverage fix (additive)**
- Keep a persistent line on screen the whole time, like "the small fits EVERYTHING," plus captions. The ad doesn't need a rescue, just a layer for people watching on mute.
