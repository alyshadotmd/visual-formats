## Pre-analysis (creative analysis skill)

**Step 0: the ad's job**
- Entry point: entertainment. Fans watch for the bit, and the product rides along.
- Persona: Jake's audience, already primed for "can you do X finding out about Y" episodes.
- Awareness: unaware to product-aware. Everyone knows Panera, so the job is a seasonal reminder (bread bowl season) plus a reason to go this week (code in caption).
- Funnel job: top-of-funnel reach and brand love, with a light conversion nudge in the caption.
- Distribution: native on the creator's own account as a paid partnership.

**Hook**
- Text: Panera's comment bubble, "can you do soup finding out about bread bowls".
- Visual: him walking into his room toward the giant fur bed.
- Spoken: "Hi, I'm Soup."
- Tactic: comment response (the brand requesting an episode like a fan) plus his series premise.
- Alignment: fully aligned. His followers know the premise from the bubble alone.

**Mechanic**
- Borrowed format: the brand rents the creator's existing series and audience expectation instead of writing a new ad. The brand shows up as a fan in the comments, so it reads as a fun request, not a sponsored script.

**Format**
- Comment Response + Signature Series (Emerging).

**Body**
- The pitch meeting is the joke: Soup is talent negotiating a deal, which quietly winks at the fact that this is a paid deal.
- The product enters as a punchline ("sample product, I'm eating myself"), then gets the only real claim: "I like the soup better than the bread."
- The closing line echoes the caption ("a lot of fun together this fall").

**What's working**
1. It's just another episode. Nothing about the shoot changed for the brand: same room, same take, same voice.
2. Panera entering through the comments makes the brand feel like part of the audience.
3. The "contract stuff... royalty-wise" line lets the joke admit it's a deal, which makes the ad feel honest and funnier.
4. The real product appears and gets eaten on camera, so the food still gets its moment.
5. The offer stays in the caption, so the episode stays pure.

**What could be stronger**
1. The real bread bowl only shows up at 0:45, and only once. A tighter shot of the soup going in would make the food more craveable.
2. Sound-off viewers only get the comment bubble. No captions carry the jokes.

**Highest-leverage fix (additive)**
- Add burned-in captions and one close-up of the bread bowl, without touching his setup or delivery.
