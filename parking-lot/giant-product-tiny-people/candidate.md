# giant-product-tiny-people (working title)

Alternative names considered: `miniature-world`, `oversized-product-scene`, `product-monument`. Undecided.

**Working definition.** Static where the product is rendered at giant / monument scale in a surreal staged scene, with miniature human figures interacting with it at ground level. The little people do double duty: scale cue plus a craft/scrutiny narrative that cashes out the headline. The composition is impossible in the real world by design.

**Nearest existing format.** `ad-in-the-wild` — but that format stages the *creative itself* as a real out-of-home billboard composited into a real street scene. Here the *product itself* is the giant object in a surreal studio scene, and there is no OOH framing at all. Different intent, different composition.

**Examples.**

- **Loop — "We take sleep seriously. So can you."** (static). Lab-coated scientists inspecting a giant Loop sleep mask, one up a ladder, one taking notes at ground level.
