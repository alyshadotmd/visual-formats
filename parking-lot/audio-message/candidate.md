# audio-message

**Working definition.** Video staged as a personal voice or audio message to a specific named person (in the seed example, the mum who gifted the product), with word-by-word captions synced to speech. The viewer overhears a private message ("Hey mum... love you, bye") rather than being pitched to. The endorsement lands because it reads as a genuine one-to-one message, not an ad to the audience. Carries a full struggle → turning-point → result arc inside that framing.

**Nearest existing format.** `testimonial` — same struggle → turning-point → result arc, but a testimonial narrates the speaker's own journey *to the audience*. Here the entire thing is second-person, addressed to the gift-giver, and the private audio-message frame is the format. Not `egc` or `founder` (no brand affiliation on the speaker), not `comment-response` (no comment sticker or reply framing).

**Examples.**

- **Spacegoods — "message to mum"** (video). Speaker records a thank-you voice message to the mum who gifted her the product, delivering her own turnaround story inside that message frame. Ad-library primary text pitches a different "depleted brain / can't focus" angle than the video itself delivers.
