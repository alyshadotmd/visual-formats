# floating-product-layer (working title)

**Working definition.** Music/try-on video where a graphical product element is composited in and floats over the live footage, previewing a look that then pays off in the real shot. No talking anchor, no script sync — the graphic is purely a visual preview device.

**Nearest existing format.** `graphic-anchor` — but that format needs a creator talking to camera with graphics matching each spoken beat. This has neither: no talking, no beat-matched graphic. Not `annotation` (callouts/marks over real footage) either, and not `greenscreen` (full-frame background). The floating product-as-preview is what makes it its own thing, if it is a thing.

**Examples.**

- **Kill Crew — "Fruit Sets Collection"** (video). Product illustrations of the fruit-print sets float across the frame over live workout footage before the wearer shows up in the actual set.
